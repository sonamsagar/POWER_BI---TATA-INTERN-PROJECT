# POWER_BI---TATA-INTERN-PROJECT
As a **Data Visualization Intern** at Tata Forge, I developed advanced dashboards to tackle critical business problems. I analyzed seasonal revenue trends for 2011 to assist the CEO with forecasting, identified top revenue-generating countries (excluding the UK) and their sales quantities for strategic decisions, and ranked the top 10 customers by revenue to help the CMO focus on high-value clients. Additionally, I evaluated regional product demand to guide the CEO's expansion strategy with a comprehensive dashboard. This work demonstrated my ability to transform complex data into actionable insights for business growth.
